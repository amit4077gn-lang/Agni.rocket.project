About AGNI Rocket

The AGNI Rocket is one of India's advanced missile systems. It is developed to provide strong defense capability and long-range strike power.

The AGNI missile series includes multiple types such as AGNI-I, AGNI-II, AGNI-III, AGNI-IV and AGNI-V. These missiles are designed with modern technology and high accuracy.

Development History

1989

First AGNI missile test conducted.

2000

Advanced AGNI versions developed.

2012

AGNI-V successfully tested.
