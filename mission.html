Mission Details

Speed

AGNI rocket travels at very high supersonic speed.

AGNI missiles can travel at several kilometers per second, making them extremely fast and capable of reaching targets in a short time.

Range

It can travel thousands of kilometers distance.

Different AGNI variants have different ranges, from hundreds to more than 5000 kilometers, allowing long-distance strike capability.

Technology

Uses advanced missile and navigation technology.

AGNI rockets use modern guidance systems, solid fuel engines, and advanced navigation technology to ensure precise flight paths.

Accuracy

Highly accurate targeting system.

The missile uses advanced control systems and navigation technology to achieve high accuracy and reliable targeting.
